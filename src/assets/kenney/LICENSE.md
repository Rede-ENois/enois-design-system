# Kenney assets

The sprites in this folder come from packs by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) and are used under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)** — the license the packs ship with. Credit is not required, but
we give it anyway.

The originals come in different sizes — from 512×512 and 1024×1024 effect sheets down to 70×70
pixel-art tiles. Here every piece is cropped to its shape, centered in a 128×128 square and saved
as WebP, keeping only an alpha silhouette on white. They are tinted at draw time with `mask-image`
(effects) or an SVG `<mask>` (banners and the scene of a ready-made look).

The app credits these packs to Kenney on the landing footer, right next to the notice about the
emotes (which come from other platforms and need a different disclaimer).
